Frequently Asked Questions

GPhC Regulated

Get answers to common questions about our medical weight loss program from our licensed pharmacists and medical specialists.

What medications are used in your weight loss program?

Our licensed pharmacists prescribe FDA-approved medications such as semaglutide, tirzepatide, and other clinically proven weight management treatments. Each prescription is tailored to your individual health profile and medical history after a comprehensive consultation.

How quickly will I see results?

Results vary by individual, but most patients begin seeing noticeable changes within 4-8 weeks of starting treatment.

Is this program covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by insurance provider. Our team can help you understand your benefits and options.

What makes your program different from other weight loss options?

Our program combines medical expertise, prescription medications, and ongoing support for sustainable results.

Are there any side effects I should be aware of?

All medications have potential side effects. Our pharmacists will discuss these with you during your consultation.

How often will I need to check in with the pharmacist?

Regular check-ins are scheduled monthly, with additional support available as needed.
